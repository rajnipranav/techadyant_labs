## 2. Minimum-touch outreach workflow
Run this cadence. Most steps are copy-paste or one-click.

### Daily 10-minute routine
1. Open Qwoted Opportunities and filter for AI/semiconductor/defence/drone/critical-minerals topics.
2. For each relevant request:
   - Save/star it
   - Copy the prepared pitch from `SEOGEO-QWOTED-CONNECTIVELY.md`
   - Personalize one sentence tied to the outlet’s beat
   - Send
   - Log in `SEOGEO-OUTREACH-LOG.md`
3. Do not pitch more than 3 per day. Quality > volume.

### Weekly 30-minute routine
1. Search Google News / Feedly for recent India semiconductor/AI/drone/recycling coverage.
2. For each article where Techadyant Labs adds evidence:
   - Send a source note to the journalist with the exact relevant page URL
   - Ask for a citation or reading-list inclusion
   - Log outcome
3. Post one LinkedIn amplification post using `GEO-LINKEDIN-PACKAGE.md` with a direct report/signal link.

## 6. Quality rules (minimal manual enforcement)
1. Never offer homepage only. Always offer a specific report/signal/research page.
2. Every outreach includes one precise reason to cite.
3. Follow up only once if relevant; no spam.
4. Log every send in `SEOGEO-OUTREACH-LOG.md` before moving on.
5. If a journalist publishes without linking, do not complain. Send a thank-you note and add them to the long-term nurture list.
